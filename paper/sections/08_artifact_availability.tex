\section{Artifact Availability}

The reproducibility artifact contains Containerlab/FRRouting configurations, schedules, measurement and analysis code, processed calibration/validation/holdout data, the frozen policy, provenance records, figures, and SHA-256 checksums. Version 1.0.0 is permanently archived on Zenodo at DOI \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21821039}{10.5281/zenodo.21821039}; the public source repository is \url{https://github.com/mo-alabdullah/ndt-ospf-candidate-link-sentinel}. The holdout is archival evidence and must not be reused for tuning.
