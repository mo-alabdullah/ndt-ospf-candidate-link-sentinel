\section{Related Work}

NDT architectures emphasize synchronization between physical and virtual networks and increasingly combine models, emulation, and automation~\cite{almasan2022ndt,hui2022digital,abenathar2025route,auge2026aether,wu2026agentndt}. MirrorNet and REAL illustrate complementary efforts to improve fidelity and scalability of network emulation~\cite{miao2026mirrornet,xia2026real}. Our focus is narrower: when a candidate routing change identifies the link that will become critical, can a targeted operational measurement compensate for a stale performance property before deployment?

This differs from OSPF weight optimization, which selects useful metrics given modeled network conditions~\cite{fortz2000ospf}, and from configuration verification, which reasons about forwarding and control-plane properties~\cite{fogel2015analysis,beckett2017verification,prabhu2020plankton}. It also complements consistent-update mechanisms that protect packet behavior during transitions~\cite{reitblatt2012updates}. A configuration can satisfy those properties and still violate a latency SLO if the operational link state is not represented by the validator.

The frozen margin used below is split-conformal-style. Classical conformal guarantees depend on exchangeability; weighted, adaptive, online, and risk-controlling variants address different forms of shift~\cite{tibshirani2019covariate,gibbs2021adaptive,gibbs2024online,bates2021risk}. We do not claim those guarantees. Instead, we explicitly report that nominal upper-bound coverage fails after residual shift, even though the binary SLO decisions remain accurate on the tested holdout.
